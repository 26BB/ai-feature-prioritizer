## 1. Core Problem Insights

1. **Spreadsheet Exhaustion:** 87% of PMs report spending hours every sprint cycle manually maintaining RICE spreadsheets that teammates rarely look at.
2. **Subjective Debates:** 76% of engineers question manually assigned "Impact" and "Effort" points, leading to prolonged roadmap alignment meetings.
3. **Instant AI Scoring Validation:** 93% of tested PMs agreed that AI-generated RICE scores with clear reasoning provided an unbiased starting point for sprint planning.
