## 1. Growth Strategy & Distribution Funnel

AI Feature Prioritizer uses a **Product-Led Growth (PLG)** distribution strategy targeting Product Managers, Product Owners, and Startup Founders.

```
FREEMIUM / BYOK DEMO (Instant Browser Try)
  └── Zero barrier entry: Try live AI RICE scoring without login.
  └── Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) for unlimited free Gemini / Llama 3 scoring.

PRODUCT-LED CONVERSION (Sign-In & Save)
  └── Sign in via Firebase Auth to persist roadmaps to Neon Serverless Postgres.
  └── 1-Click export to CSV or copy Jira/Linear formatted markdown tables.

VIRAL ADVOCACY & COMMUNITY DISTRIBUTION
  └── Shareable public roadmap links for stakeholder review.
  └── Product Hunt & LinkedIn PM community showcases.
```

## 2. Growth Loops & Retention Drivers

1. **The BYOK Value Hook:** Users can enter their own free Google Gemini API key to use high-tier AI models without paying a monthly subscription.
2. **CSV & Linear Export Loop:** Product managers export their RICE scores directly into team sprint planning tools (Jira, Linear, Notion), creating habit-forming weekly retention.
